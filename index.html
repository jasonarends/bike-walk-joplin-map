How This Map Works

01

Official JATSO Data

This map loads live data from the JATSO Bike & Pedestrian Plan — a formal effort by the Joplin Area Transportation Study Organization with contractor Crafton Tull. Those points and routes are shown in purple.

02

Community Reports

Use the "Report an Issue" button to submit your own observations — problem spots, route ideas, or places already working well. Submissions go directly to Bike Walk Joplin.

03

BWJ Advocacy

Bike Walk Joplin uses this data to advocate with city officials, prioritize improvements, and build the case for a safer, more connected Joplin for everyone.

04

Open Data

All JATSO data is publicly accessible via ArcGIS REST API. This map refreshes live — you always see the latest submissions from both the official process and our community.
